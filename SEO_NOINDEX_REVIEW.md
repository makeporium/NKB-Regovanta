# Noindex review and implementation

Reviewed 7 September 2026. Local changes; deployment and actual Google indexing are not asserted.

## Result

Reviewed all 193 previously excluded content routes against their source content, related pages and the supplied Search Console Pages export. Restored 158 pages; retained 35 exclusions. The site now has 230 indexable content pages and 35 noindex pages. All 230 indexable canonical URLs are in the sitemap. All 32 original replacement articles now allow indexing.

## Decision method

Read route content and embedded arrays, checked shared components and original article JSON, compared eight-word sequences across all 265 content pages, and reviewed purpose and depth. Shared styling, repeated navigation, country-hub summaries, absence of impressions and short word counts are not automatic reasons to exclude a page. Distinct country requirements, audiences, service deliverables and informational questions can justify separate pages. Similarity scores were review aids, not Google metrics or an automatic indexing threshold.

The supplied export has no reporting dates or filters; its impressions are historical evidence of visibility, not proof of current indexing. Page and query exports cannot be joined to prove a specific query-to-page relationship. The CSV records page-level observations only.

## Exclusions retained

- accuracy-review: 1
- duplicate-feed: 1
- navigation-bridge: 4
- outdated-regulator: 14
- overlapping-content: 1
- placeholder: 11
- underdeveloped: 3

These exclusions are temporary content decisions, not a claim that noindex consolidates ranking signals. True duplicate/bridge URLs should eventually be consolidated using intentional redirects or canonicalisation after selecting the destination. URLs and existing links are preserved in this change.

## Corrections made before restoring indexing

- US IVD: corrected the cancelled LDT-rule phase-in, CLIA waiver explanation and QMSR effective-date wording.
- EU IVD: corrected Class C versus Class D EURL involvement, EUDAMED timing language and legacy scope; removed unsupported benchmark certification prices and assumed submission languages.
- Cosmetics: qualified MoCRA exemptions, separated Great Britain from Northern Ireland, and clarified that CPNP notification is not approval.
- Neutral Code: clarified export eligibility and added the CDSCO system-generated-code notice.
- EU Article 117: qualified when a Notified Body opinion is required.
- EU labelling and deficiency response: removed a verbatim-wording requirement and unsupported universal response deadlines.
- MHRA registration: replaced the old fixed fee with the current fee guidance.
- eSTAR: removed the promise of zero screening rejections.

UAE detail pages remain excluded because they still describe MOHAP as the current medical-device interface. EDE now provides the relevant services. The India IVD overview remains excluded pending verification of its blanket local-testing claims. PMA remains excluded because it repeats De Novo eligibility and shares substantial body content. These require substantive follow-up; this review is not a comprehensive legal or clinical certification of every website statement.

## Validation

Passed: source SEO checks for all 265 pages; article integrity and Linux/Windows hash consistency for all 32 replacements; reviewed indexing decisions; TypeScript; all seven regression tests; rendered HTML checks across all 265 pages; and the production build. The rendered checker verifies canonical URLs, one H1, metadata, indexing state and replacement article paragraphs. Build output is recorded in .seo-build-verification.log.
