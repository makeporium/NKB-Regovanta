## CDSCO Manufacturing License for Medical Devices in India: Registration, SUGAM Portal, Documents, Process &#038; Compliance Guide (2026)

India has emerged as one of the fastest-growing medical device manufacturing hubs globally. With increasing investments in healthcare infrastructure, government support through the "Make in India" initiative, and growing domestic demand, numerous 

entrepreneurs and companies are establishing medical device manufacturing facilities across the country.

However, before manufacturing and marketing medical devices in India, obtaining the appropriate CDSCO Manufacturing License for Medical Devices is a mandatory regulatory requirement under the Medical Devices Rules (MDR), 2017.

If you are planning to establish a medical device manufacturing facility, understanding CDSCO registration, CDSCO manufacturing license requirements, CDSCO SUGAM portal procedures, facility compliance, documentation, and quality management systems is essential for successful market entry.

This comprehensive FAQ guide answers the most common questions asked by medical device manufacturers and investors.

## What is CDSCO?

CDSCO (Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation) is the National Regulatory Authority of India operating under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India.

The Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) is responsible for regulating:

* Medical Devices
* In-Vitro Diagnostic Devices (IVDs)
* Pharmaceuticals
* Cosmetics
* Clinical Investigations

The primary objective of CDSCO is to ensure that products manufactured and marketed in India are safe, effective, and meet established quality standards.

## What is CDSCO Registration?

CDSCO Registration is the regulatory process through which medical device manufacturers obtain approval to manufacture, import, sell, or distribute regulated medical devices in India.

For manufacturers, CDSCO registration typically involves:

* Medical device classification
* Facility compliance assessment
* Technical documentation review
* Quality management system implementation
* Manufacturing license approval

Without appropriate CDSCO registration and licensing, manufacturing and commercial distribution of regulated medical devices is not permitted.

Why is a CDSCO Manufacturing License Important?

A CDSCO Manufacturing License is the legal authorization required to manufacture medical devices in India.

The license confirms that:

* The manufacturing facility complies with regulatory requirements.
* The company has qualified technical personnel.
* Quality management systems are established.
* Manufacturing processes meet applicable standards.
* Products are manufactured under controlled conditions.

For companies investing in medical device manufacturing facilities, obtaining the manufacturing license is one of the most critical milestones before commercial production can begin.

## Who Requires a CDSCO Manufacturing License for Medical Devices?

The following entities generally require a CDSCO manufacturing license for medical devices :

* Medical Device Manufacturers
* Disposable Medical Device Manufacturers
* Implant Manufacturers
* In-Vitro Diagnostic (IVD) Manufacturers
* Contract Manufacturers
* OEM Manufacturers
* Private Label Manufacturers
* Startups Establishing Medical Device Manufacturing Facilities

Whether you manufacture syringes, IV cannulas, infusion sets, blood collection tubes, surgical sutures, orthopedic implants, diagnostic kits, or electronic medical devices, regulatory compliance is essential.

What is the CDSCO SUGAM Portal?

The CDSCO SUGAM Portal is the official online regulatory platform used for:

* CDSCO Online Registration
* Medical Device Registration
* Manufacturing License Applications
* Import License Applications
* Regulatory Communication
* Application Tracking

Today, virtually all CDSCO medical device applications are submitted through the SUGAM portal.

## What Documents Are Required for CDSCO Manufacturing License?

The documentation requirements vary depending on device classification but generally include:

Device Master File (DMF)

The Device Master File contains:

* Device Description
* Intended Use
* Product Specifications
* Design Information
* Risk Analysis
* Performance Evaluation Data
* Testing Reports
* Labeling Information

Site Master File (SMF)

The Site Master File contains:

* Manufacturing Facility Information
* Facility Layout
* Cleanroom Details
* Utilities
* Equipment Information
* Quality Management System Structure

Quality System Documentation

* Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
* Validation Reports
* Calibration Records
* Training Records
* Supplier Qualification Documents
* Change Control Procedures

Additional Supporting Documents

* Company Registration Documents
* Technical Personnel Qualifications
* Manufacturing Process Flowcharts
* Product Specifications
* Testing Reports

## What is the CDSCO Manufacturing License Process?

Step 1: Device Classification

Determine whether the product falls under Class A, B, C, or D.

Step 2: Facility Planning and Compliance Assessment

Establish a manufacturing facility that meets applicable regulatory requirements.

Step 3: Documentation Preparation

Prepare Device Master Files, Site Master Files, quality documentation, and technical records.

Step 4: CDSCO Online Registration

Register on the CDSCO SUGAM Portal.

Step 5: Application Submission

Submit the applicable forms and supporting documentation.

Step 6: Regulatory Review

Authorities review technical and quality documentation.

Step 7: Facility Audit or Inspection

An audit may be conducted depending on the device classification and regulatory requirements.

Step 8: License Approval

Upon successful review, the CDSCO Manufacturing License is granted.

## What are the Common Challenges Faced by Medical Device Manufacturers?

Manufacturers frequently encounter:

Incorrect Device Classification

Improper classification can delay approvals and increase compliance risks.

Incomplete Documentation

Missing Device Master Files, Site Master Files, or testing reports often lead to regulatory queries.

Poor Facility Design

Improper layouts can create operational inefficiencies and regulatory concerns.

Weak Quality Management Systems

Lack of structured quality processes can impact approval success.

Delayed Regulatory Planning

Waiting until facility completion to address compliance requirements often causes avoidable delays.
